This week adds 250 words in eight thematic groups plus a word-formation pattern, bringing the cumulative total to **1,150 words**. The groups are chosen for the B1 topics you will discuss and read about: society, environment, education, opinions, media, health, travel, and digital life. Nouns carry their article and plural form; verbs are given as infinitives; adjective phrases are listed under the group they belong to.
