This week adds 300 words in nine thematic groups plus a word-formation pattern, bringing the cumulative total to **1,450 words**. The groups prepare you for B1 conversations about public life: politics, economics, work, bureaucracy, global issues, and the language of feelings. Nouns carry their article and plural form; verbs are given as infinitives; adjective phrases are listed under the group they belong to.
