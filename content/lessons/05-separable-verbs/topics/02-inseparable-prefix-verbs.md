### 2.1. The Inseparable Prefixes

**Inseparable prefixes** never separate from the verb stem. In main clauses the whole verb stays together in position 2; in the Perfekt there is **no ge-**. The prefixes are **always unstressed**.

| Prefix | Meaning / Function | Example Verb | Meaning |
|--------|--------------------|--------------|---------|
| be- | creates verbs (often makes a verb transitive) | bekommen, besuchen | to get, to visit |
| emp- | receiving, feeling | empfehlen, empfangen | to recommend, to receive |
| ent- | away from, un-, de- | entscheiden, entfernen | to decide, to remove |
| er- | result or completion of the action | erklären, erinnern, erhalten | to explain, to remember, to receive |
| ge- | (verb-forming prefix) | gehören, gefallen | to belong, to please |
| miss- | mis-, wrong | missverstehen | to misunderstand |
| ver- | change, away, intensification | verstehen, vergessen, versprechen | to understand, to forget, to promise |
| zer- | to pieces, destruction | zerstören | to destroy |

> **Note:** ge- is ambiguous — it appears as a real inseparable prefix (gehören) AND as the marker of the Partizip II (gehen → ge**gangen**). Do not confuse the two: the participle ge- is part of the past-participle formation system; the prefix ge- is part of the verb itself (gehören has no plain stem hören).

### 2.2. No ge- in the Partizip II

Because the prefix is permanently glued to the stem, there is **no room for ge-** in the Partizip II — and no need: the prefix itself already signals the participle.

**10 examples (Infinitiv → Partizip II):**

| Infinitive | Partizip II | Perfekt Example | English |
|------------|-------------|-----------------|---------|
| verstehen | verstanden | Ich habe die Frage verstanden. | I understood the question. |
| bekommen | bekommen | Er hat ein Geschenk bekommen. | He received a gift. |
| empfehlen | empfohlen | Sie hat den Film empfohlen. | She recommended the film. |
| erzählen | erzählt | Ich habe eine Geschichte erzählt. | I told a story. |
| beginnen | begonnen | Der Kurs hat begonnen. | The course has begun. |
| entscheiden | entschieden | Wir haben uns entschieden. | We have decided. |
| vergessen | vergessen | Ich habe den Schlüssel vergessen. | I forgot the key. |
| zerstören | zerstört | Der Sturm hat das Haus zerstört. | The storm destroyed the house. |
| entdecken | entdeckt | Er hat eine neue Idee entdeckt. | He discovered a new idea. |
| versprechen | versprochen | Sie hat es versprochen. | She promised it. |

> **Contrast with separable (Lesson 2, §4):** aufstehen → auf**ge**standen (ge- between prefix and stem), but verstehen → **ver**standen (no ge- at all). When you meet a prefix verb, ask first: separable or inseparable? That decides everything about the participle.

### 2.4. The Stress Rule: Separable vs. Inseparable

The fastest way to tell the two classes apart is **stress**. In a separable verb the **prefix is stressed**; in an inseparable verb the **stem is stressed** (the prefix is unstressed).

| Type | Stressed syllable | Example |
|------|-------------------|---------|
| Separable | **prefix** | **AUF**stehen, **EIN**kaufen, **AN**fangen, **MIT**nehmen |
| Inseparable | **stem** | ver**STE**hen, be**KOM**men, er**KLÄ**ren, ver**GES**sen |

**Worked examples:**

- **ÁNfangen** (separable) — the a of an- is stressed → separable → participle *angefangen*, prefix separates: *Ich fange an.*
- **anFÁNGEN** would be wrong: there is no such verb with stressed stem here — an inseparable an- does not exist (an- is always separable).
- **verSTEHen** (inseparable) — the stem steh- is stressed → inseparable → participle *verstanden* (no ge-), never separates: *Ich verstehe.*
- **VERstehen** (stressed ver-) would be wrong: no such verb exists.

**Three quick tests for any prefix verb:**

1. **Stress test:** prefix stressed → separable; stem stressed → inseparable.
2. **ge- test:** participle has ge- between prefix and stem → separable; participle has no ge- → inseparable.
3. **End-position test:** in a main clause the prefix can go to the end → separable; it stays glued → inseparable.

> **Note:** Verbs ending in **-ieren** (telefonieren, studieren, probieren) take **no ge-** either — but they are neither separable nor inseparable; they are plain weak verbs that simply follow the -ieren rule (Lesson 2, §4). Do not confuse them with prefix verbs.
