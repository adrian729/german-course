This week adds **350 words** in nine thematic groups plus a word-formation pattern, bringing the cumulative total to **2,100 words**. The groups serve the Lesson 9 topics: news and reporting (for the indirect-speech reading in §7.1), the academic and workplace registers (for summaries and meetings), and the social, cultural, and technical worlds of a B1 speaker.
