This week adds **400 words** in eleven thematic groups, bringing the cumulative total to **2,900 words**. The groups serve the Lesson 11 topics: the formal, academic, scientific, legal, and journalistic vocabulary you need to read and write at B2 — exactly the registers that Konjunktiv I, the N-Deklination, and the Nominalstil belong to. Nouns carry their article and plural form; verbs are given as infinitives; phrases are listed under the group they belong to.
