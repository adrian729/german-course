In Lesson 7 you met the **Vorgangspassiv** (process passive): *Das Fenster **wird** geöffnet* — it reports an action in progress. This week you learn its sibling, the **Zustandspassiv** (state passive): *Das Fenster **ist** geöffnet* — it reports the result, the state that exists after the action. Where the Vorgangspassiv answers "what is happening to X?", the Zustandspassiv answers "in what state is X?". The distinction is the single most important passive idea at B2, because German uses both forms side by side every day.

### 1.1. Formation: sein + Partizip II

The Zustandspassiv is built exactly like a sein sentence: **sein (conjugated) + Partizip II at the end**. The verb werden does not appear — that is the whole contrast with the Vorgangspassiv. The subject of a Zustandspassiv is the object of the action, and the doer is almost never named, because the point is the state, not the action.

**3 formation examples:**

1. Die Tür **ist geschlossen**. (The door is closed.)
2. Der Brief **ist geschrieben**. (The letter is written.)
3. Das Problem **ist gelöst**. (The problem is solved.)

> **Trap (critical):** the Zustandspassiv looks identical to the Perfekt of a sein-verb (*Er ist gegangen*), but it is not. The test is *worden*: *Die Tür ist geöffnet **worden*** is Vorgangspassiv in Perfekt (the action took place); *Die Tür ist geöffnet* alone is Zustandspassiv (the state exists now).

### 1.2. Vorgangspassiv vs. Zustandspassiv: 5 Contrastive Pairs

**5 contrastive pairs:**

| Vorgangspassiv (action) | Zustandspassiv (state) | English |
|-------------------------|-------------------------|---------|
| Das Fenster **wird** geöffnet. | Das Fenster **ist** geöffnet. | The window is being opened. / The window is open. |
| Die Tür **wird** geschlossen. | Die Tür **ist** geschlossen. | The door is being closed. / The door is closed. |
| Der Brief **wird** geschrieben. | Der Brief **ist** geschrieben. | The letter is being written. / The letter is written. |
| Der Kuchen **wird** gebacken. | Der Kuchen **ist** gebacken. | The cake is being baked. / The cake is baked. |
| Das Licht **wird** ausgeschaltet. | Das Licht **ist** ausgeschaltet. | The light is being switched off. / The light is off. |

> **Decision test:** ask yourself "is something happening right now?" — that is Vorgangspassiv; or "has the action been completed and a state exists?" — that is Zustandspassiv. *Die Tür wird geschlossen* means someone is closing it at this moment; *Die Tür ist geschlossen* means it was closed before and remains closed.

### 1.3. Zustandspassiv Across Tenses: Chart and 3 Examples

The Zustandspassiv can be conjugated in every tense by putting sein into the right tense. Note the Futur carefully: **wird + Partizip II + sein**.

**Chart across tenses (das Fenster, geöffnet):**

| Tense | Form | Example |
|-------|------|---------|
| Präsens | ist + geöffnet | Das Fenster **ist geöffnet**. |
| Präteritum | war + geöffnet | Das Fenster **war geöffnet**. |
| Perfekt | ist + geöffnet gewesen | Das Fenster **ist geöffnet gewesen**. |
| Plusquamperfekt | war + geöffnet gewesen | Das Fenster **war geöffnet gewesen**. |
| Futur I | wird + geöffnet sein | Das Fenster **wird geöffnet sein**. |

**3 examples:**

1. Die Bibliothek **ist** heute **geschlossen**. (The library is closed today. — Präsens)
2. Als wir ankamen, **war** die Tür schon **geschlossen**. (When we arrived, the door was already closed. — Präteritum)
3. Morgen um neun **wird** das Tor **geöffnet sein**. (Tomorrow at nine the gate will be open. — Futur)

> **Futur trap:** the Futur of the Zustandspassiv stacks **werden + sein**: *wird geöffnet sein*. Never write ~~wird geöffnet werden sein~~ — that mixes the Vorgangspassiv into a Zustandspassiv future and is wrong.
