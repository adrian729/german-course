This week adds 400 words in eleven thematic groups plus a word-formation pattern, bringing the cumulative total to **2,500 words**. The groups serve the B2 topics of this week: law, business, formal correspondence, idioms, science, philosophy, diplomacy, health policy, the arts, social sciences, and environmental policy. Nouns carry their article and plural form; verbs are given as infinitives; phrases and idioms are listed under the group they belong to.
