This final week adds **400 words** in ten groups, bringing the cumulative total to **3,300 words**. The first four groups are the finishing layer of the B2 lexicon: idioms and proverbs, register variation, false friends, and the academic-professional core. The next five groups (9.5–9.9) are the **comprehensive review** of the entire course, thematically organised — every word below has appeared in Lessons 1–11 and is now consolidated for exam readiness. Nouns carry their article and plural form; verbs are given as infinitives; phrases are listed under the group they belong to.
