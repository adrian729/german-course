This week adds 300 words in nine thematic groups plus a word-formation pattern, bringing the cumulative total to **1,750 words**. The groups serve the B1 topics of this week: culture, science, consumer life, health, education, urban life, and media — plus the formal register needed for the writing task in §7.2. Nouns carry their article and plural form; verbs are given as infinitives; adjective phrases are listed under the group they belong to.
